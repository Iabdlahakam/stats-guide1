# Brand Accounts and Content System

## Accounts to Create

Create consistent handles, profile photos, descriptions, and links across platforms.

Recommended first accounts:

- Professional website/domain
- Business email
- LinkedIn company page
- LinkedIn founder profile refresh
- YouTube channel
- X/Twitter account
- Instagram account
- Facebook page or group if your audience uses it
- Calendly or booking link
- Stripe or payment link

## Profile Bio Template

> AI-powered research support for residents, fellows, and students. We help turn clinical ideas into protocols, data plans, analysis plans, and publication roadmaps.

## Weekly Content Rhythm

- 1 educational YouTube video
- 2 LinkedIn posts
- 2 short-form posts or clips
- 1 client-focused case example using fictional/anonymized details
- 1 call-to-action post inviting intake form submissions

## Content Pillars

- Research idea development
- Study design basics
- Protocol writing

- Data collection and data quality
- Statistics planning
