# Brand Accounts and Content System

## Account Setup Priority

Create the accounts in this order so the brand looks consistent before content is promoted.

1. Domain and website for Clinical Research Compass.
2. Professional email address using the domain.
3. LinkedIn company page.
4. Founder LinkedIn profile refresh.
5. YouTube channel.
6. Instagram account.
7. X/Twitter account if you can maintain it consistently.
8. Facebook page or group only if your audience uses it.
9. Calendly or booking link.
10. Stripe or payment link.

## Profile Bio Template

> Clinical Research Compass provides expert, independent guidance to strengthen the design, interpretation, and impact of clinical research. Practical support for research questions, protocols, data plans, and analysis planning.

## Brand Voice for Posts

Use a tone that is clear, precise, calm, clinically grounded, and evidence-respecting. Sound like a trusted senior clinical research advisor.

Avoid hype, casual language, “ultimate guide,” “game-changing,” or overly academic jargon. Use practical but professional phrasing.

## First Content Model

Each post should be a full educational explainer, not just a quote or short tip.

1. Short clinical hook.
2. Definition.
3. Key components.
4. Step-by-step use.
5. Common mistakes.
6. Worked example.
7. Practical template or checklist gift.
8. Signature CTA: **“Comment COMPASS for the worksheet.”**

## First Content Series: Research Question Frameworks

### Post 1: PICOT Framework

**Core example:** Adults with type 2 diabetes; telemedicine follow-up versus standard in-person care; HbA1c over 6 months.

**Deliverables:**

- Full explanatory caption.
- Mind-map or infographic image.
- Branded footer.
- Light repeating watermark.
- Downloadable PICOT Research Question Checklist / Worksheet.

### Post 2: PEO Framework

Use this post to explain when a research question does not require a formal comparison group, especially for qualitative, exploratory, exposure, and experience-focused projects.

### Post 3: SPIDER Framework

Use this post to support qualitative and mixed-methods questions where the sample, phenomenon, design, evaluation, and research type need to be explicit.

## Weekly Content Rhythm

- 1 educational YouTube video or long-form LinkedIn article.
- 2 LinkedIn educational posts.
- 2 short-form posts or clips.
- 1 worksheet, checklist, or carousel from the Research Question Starter Pack.
- 1 call-to-action post inviting comments, worksheet requests, or intake form submissions.
