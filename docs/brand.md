# Clinical Research Compass Brand System

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Clinical Research Compass  
**Positioning:** Clinical research consultancy and guidance platform.  
**Official mission statement:** “To provide expert, independent guidance that strengthens the design, interpretation, and impact of clinical research.”  
**Short public tagline:** “Expert guidance for rigorous and impactful clinical research.”

## What the Brand Should Mean

Clinical Research Compass should feel like a trusted senior research advisor: calm, precise, clinically grounded, and practical. The brand should help residents, fellows, students, and clinical teams move from uncertainty to a clear research path without overpromising or replacing institutional review.

## Logo Rules

Use the flat CRC compass logo as the master logo across the website, posts, downloadable worksheets, slide decks, and client-facing tools.

**Use:**

- CRC letters inside a compass.
- Navy and teal as the primary colors.
- Flat, clean styling.
- Consistent placement in document headers, footers, and social images.
- A light repeating watermark across educational images and downloadable tools.

**Avoid:**

- Hazy 3D effects.
- Glow, blur, heavy shadows, or metallic effects.
- Overly promotional or “tech startup hype” styling.
- Changing the logo colors without a clear accessibility reason.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Primary headings, header bars, main logo color |
| Teal | `#0F9D9A` | Accent lines, links, calls to action, compass points |
| Soft teal background | `#F4FBFB` | Educational cards, worksheet sections, callouts |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean document background |

## Brand Voice

Clinical Research Compass should sound:

- Clear.
- Precise.
- Calm.
- Clinically grounded.
- Evidence-respecting.
- Practical but professional.

Avoid:

- Hype language such as “ultimate,” “game-changing,” or “revolutionary.”
- Casual slang.
- Overly academic jargon when plain clinical language is enough.
- Guarantees of approval, publication, or statistical significance.

## Standard Disclaimer Language

Use this disclaimer on educational content, worksheets, and service pages:

> Clinical Research Compass provides educational research guidance and planning support. It does not replace institutional review board review, legal or regulatory advice, clinical judgment, or formal statistical consultation for complex or high-risk studies.
